Stick privacy policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Developer: Sidharth Sreekumar

Summary

Stick is a habit app that keeps everything on your phone. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I, the developer, never see your data.

What Stick stores

Only what you enter or create in the app:

  • your habit: its name, emoji, tiny version, your reason for starting and your reminder time;
  • your answers to the two setup questions (when and why you usually stop);
  • the days you checked in, your attempts and the app's settings;
  • voice and video clips you choose to record as messages to your future self.

Where it is stored

On your phone only, in Stick's private app storage. Stick does not upload, share or sell any of it, and it does not use any third-party service that collects data.

Stick also reads your phone's maker and time zone, on the phone, to show the right battery-saver tip and to time your reminders. Neither is stored or sent anywhere.

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall Stick.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: only while you record a clip. Nothing is recorded in the background.
  • Notifications: to show your daily reminder. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration and keep-awake: so reminders can vibrate and arrive reliably.
  • Network state: this permission is added by one of the libraries Stick uses. Stick has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to anything or send anything.

Android backup

If Android's own backup is turned on for your phone, Google's device backup may include Stick's habit data (not your clips) in your Google account, under Google's terms. Moving to a new phone with Android's transfer tool may copy everything, clips included. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings. I have no access to these backups.

Deleting your data

  • In Stick, Settings → Fresh start clears your habit and your days (you choose whether to keep your clips).
  • In the Messages tab you can delete any clip.
  • Uninstalling Stick deletes everything it stored on your phone.

Purchases

A future one-time Pro unlock will be sold through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; Stick never sees your payment details.

Children

Stick is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
